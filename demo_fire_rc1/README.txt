==============================================================================
DEMO TITLE : Fire Demo
DEMO NAME  : fire_rc1
FOLDER     : demo_fire_rc1
==============================================================================

ABOUT
-----
Double-LORES fire simulation with dynamic masking over a scrolling banner,
plus MockingBoard music. Needs an Apple //e (65C02), 1 MHz, MockingBoard or
compatible.

FILES REVIEWED
--------------
demo_fire_rc1.dsk (DOS 3.3): HELLO (Applesoft launcher), FIRE (machine code),
SONG (PT3 music).

ATTRIBUTION
-----------
  * Uses the DOLORes double-LORES library.
  * "Merry X-mas 2020 and a happy new year!" and the tag "8-BIT-SHACK" appear
    in the demo.
  * Published by 8-Bit Shack.

MUSIC
-----
  * PT3 tune ".traxmo.version.o1. 1752270802.1" by Cj Echo (Aleksandr
    Maksimov) of the group Triumph.

THIRD-PARTY SOFTWARE / MATERIAL
-------------------------------
