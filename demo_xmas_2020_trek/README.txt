==============================================================================
DEMO TITLE : Christmas 2020 Demo (Star Trek parody)
DEMO NAME  : xmas_2020_trek
FOLDER     : demo_xmas_2020_trek
==============================================================================

ABOUT
-----
Humorous Star Trek-inspired Christmas animation with an unmistakable 1980s
flavor.

FILES REVIEWED
--------------
demo_xmas_2020_trek.po (ProDOS volume /BLANK): STARTUP, MAIN, INTRO.FRAMES,
TREK.FRAMES, TREK.SPRITES, PLAYER, BEATIT.SAMPLE, music files M.*, GREASE,
GRIDDLE, SLICK.

ATTRIBUTION
-----------
  * Star Trek parody by Sellam Abraham and Dan Henderson.
  * GReasy / Greasy Graphics lo-res sprite and animation library: Sellam
    Abraham.
  * Star Trek (Paramount) and the Back to the Future DeLorean (Universal) are
    referenced as parody; no material from them is included.

MUSIC
-----
  * "Deck the Halls" (traditional Welsh melody, public domain); "Jingle Bells"
    (James Lord Pierpont, 1857, public domain); "Silver Bells" (Jay Livingston
    and Ray Evans, 1950, copyrighted); a digitized sample of "Beat It"
    (Michael Jackson, 1982, copyrighted).
