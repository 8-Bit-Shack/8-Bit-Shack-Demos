DEMO TITLE : Christmas 2024 Demo (Penguins)
DEMO NAME  : xmas_2024
FOLDER     : demo_xmas_2024
==============================================================================

ABOUT
-----
Playful Christmas animation featuring dancing penguins and wintertime antics
(double LORES, MockingBoard).

FILES REVIEWED
--------------
demo_xmas_2024.po (ProDOS volume /XMAS24): STARTUP, DEMO, PEN1-PEN10 (frames),
SONG, AUXINIT, AUXPLAY, GREASE, GRIDDLE, SLICK.

ATTRIBUTION
-----------
  * "Penguins - Christmas 2024" by 8 Bit Shack; "Apple II Forever" by Dan
    Henderson, 2024.
  * GReasy / Greasy Graphics lo-res sprite and animation library: Sellam
    Abraham.
  * Demo loader and PT3 player: Marc A. Golombeck's FFEdit Demo Loader
    toolchain.

MUSIC
-----
  * PT3 tune "White Xmas" by z00m of TCG, November 2003 (ProTracker 3.5
    module).
