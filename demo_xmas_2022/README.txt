==============================================================================
DEMO TITLE : Christmas 2022 Demo (A Knight's Tale 2022)
DEMO NAME  : xmas_2022
FOLDER     : demo_xmas_2022
==============================================================================

ABOUT
-----
Medieval animated short in double LORES with MockingBoard music (requires
Double LORES; MockingBoard for sound).

FILES REVIEWED
--------------
demo_xmas_2022.po (ProDOS volume /CHRISTMAS2022): STARTUP, LOADER, DEMO, SONG,
AUXINIT, AUXPLAY, FRAMEANIM and ~25 frame files (KNIGHT, DRUNKNIGHT1-4,
BORED1-4, INTRO1-3, SLASH1-3, LIZARD, ...).

ATTRIBUTION
-----------
  * By Dan Henderson, created with Marc A. Golombeck's FF Edit and Demo Loader
    (with FrameAnim and a PT3 player).
  * FFEdit, Demo Loader and FrameAnim: Marc A. Golombeck (the loader files and
    PT3 music player come from that toolchain).
