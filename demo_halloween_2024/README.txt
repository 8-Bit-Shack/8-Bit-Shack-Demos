DEMO TITLE : Halloween 2024 Demo
DEMO NAME  : halloween_2024
FOLDER     : demo_halloween_2024
==============================================================================

ABOUT
-----
Animated tribute to The Shining, featuring iconic scenes and spooky music.

FILES REVIEWED
--------------
demo_halloween_2024.po (ProDOS volume /FFEDIT): STARTUP, LOADER, DEMO,
FRAMES/SHIN1-SHIN11 (double-lo-res frames), SONG, AUXINIT, AUXPLAY, FRAMEANIM,
FRED, SPLASH.

ATTRIBUTION
-----------
  * Published by 8-Bit Shack. An animated rendition of Stanley Kubrick's The
    Shining (1980 film, based on the novel by Stephen King; referenced, not
    included).
  * FFEdit, Demo Loader and FrameAnim: Marc A. Golombeck (the loader files and
    PT3 music player come from that toolchain).

MUSIC
-----
  * PT3 tune "SCARY MUZIK" by SAM, summer 2005 (ProTracker 3.6 module).
