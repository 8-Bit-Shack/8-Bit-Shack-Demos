ABOUT
-----
Christmas themed rotozoomer animation with Mockingboard music.

FILES REVIEWED
--------------
demo_xmas_2018.dsk (DOS 3.3): HELLO, MB (MockingBoard player/detection), SONG,
SHACK2 and SHACKLZ (graphics), BOSSLZ.
